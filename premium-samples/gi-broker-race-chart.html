5 FINDINGS
Movement patterns across the top 20 AFSLs over the 24-month window.
  1. Resilium is in sustained decline — down 137 ARs (−22%) over 24 months, falling in 14 of 23 months. McLardy McShane has overtaken them for #3.
  2. PSC Connect is shrinking at the same rate — down 82 (−26%), falling in 16 of 23 months. Both networks are being sold by Envest to Steadfast.
  3. The decline was visible long before the deal. PSC lost access to Steadfast's Client Trading Platform (SCTP) in mid-2023 after leaving the network. AR attrition at both Resilium and PSC Connect accelerated from there — well before the April 2026 Steadfast acquisition was announced.
  4. CBN dominates but may have peaked — grew from 879 to 1,115 (+27%), but has dropped 26 since hitting a high of 1,141 in Feb 2026.
  5. McLardy McShane is the steadiest grower — up 175 (+46%), positive in 19 of 23 months. Remarkably consistent.
