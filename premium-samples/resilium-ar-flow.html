What the data shows

  • Resilium lost 20% of its AR/CAR network over 4 years — 615 ARs in May 2022, 492 by April 2026. Last week Steadfast announced it will acquire Resilium and PSC Connect from Envest, integrating both into Ausure.
  • The transition didn't start last week — it's been happening for years. 540 ARs left and 413 joined over 48 months. That level of turnover through a ~500-600 person network is significant.
  • The Steadfast ecosystem was already the destination. Ausure (net -38), McLardy McShane (net -36), and Community Broker Network (net -32) together account for 123 identified departures — exactly equal to Resilium's total net headcount loss.
  • December and January are consistently the worst months — likely driven by end-of-year contract cycles. January 2025 alone saw 49 departures.
  • January 2024 anomaly: +63 joiners in a single month — looks like a bulk transfer, but the gains were gradually eroded through the rest of 2024 and into 2025.
