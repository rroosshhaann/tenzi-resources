Data notes

The ranking counts authorised representatives listed on the ASIC AFS Authorised Representative register at 30 June 2026, by general-insurance licensee. Source: ASIC AFS Licensee and Authorised Representative registers (data.gov.au). The registers are public but not built for analysis over time, so Tenzi reconciles the monthly snapshots across 48 months, matches people across licence changes, and separates genuine movement from re-papering, restructures and dual appointments before counting. The full methodology sits in the report; the metric review shows the same 30 June population with its tenure, growth and flow measures, and is refreshed as new register data lands. The register says what moved. For why, read the report.
