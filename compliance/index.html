Reading the guide04 / 06

Understanding the draft categories

These are working descriptions to help you browse. The allocation of legal responsibility needs expert review.

  • Direct AR: entries describing a possible duty on the representative or service provider.
  • Licensee-led: entries describing the licensee’s responsibilities and oversight.
  • Shared: entries describing activities involving both the AR and its network.

Coverage and review

  • This initial directory focuses on selected Chapter 7 and ASIC topics for GI ARs.
  • Other laws, industry codes, licence conditions, AR agreements and network procedures may also matter.
  • Linked legislation and regulator guidance have different legal status. Our summaries are separate, unverified interpretations.
  • Source link dates record destination checks only. Source currency, exceptions and completeness still need checking.
